Daily Macro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

What the app is

Daily Macro is a private script that runs on its owner's own computer. Once a day it composes a market briefing and sends it by email. It has a single user: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

The app requests one Google permission only: gmail.send, which allows it to send email from the owner's Gmail account. It cannot read, search, modify or delete any email, contact, calendar entry or other Google data, and it does not request any permission that would allow it to.

What it stores

The authorisation token issued by Google is stored only on the owner's computer, to send the daily email. No Google user data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone, and nothing is sent to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
